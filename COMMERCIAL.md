# Commercial licensing

`agent-studio` is released under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**. The AGPL's network-use
clause requires that anyone offering the software as a network service make the
complete corresponding source available to its users.

If those terms do not fit your use — for example you want to embed
`agent-studio` in a closed-source product or offer it as a hosted service
without releasing your modifications — a **commercial license** is available.

A commercial license grants:

- use without the AGPL source-disclosure obligations,
- the right to distribute/host modified versions privately,
- priority support and integration guidance for the platform seams
  (MCP Gateway, LLM Bridge, OpenFGA/SpiceDB).

**Contact:** open an issue titled `commercial-license` or email the maintainers
listed in `CITATION`/repo metadata.

This dual-licensing model keeps the community edition fully open while funding
continued development.
